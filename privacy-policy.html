1. About JB STORE

JB STORE is an electronics shopping application and website operated by OMG_X_NOOR. The service allows users to browse products, use an account or Guest Login, save profile and delivery information, maintain a shopping cart and favorites list, view orders, and submit order information to JB STORE through the available checkout flow.

2. Information We Collect or Process

Depending on how you use JB STORE, the current implementation processes the following categories of information:

Category Examples in JB STORE Why it is used
Account & authentication Email address, Supabase user/account identifier, authentication session information, and sign-in method. To create, authenticate, maintain and secure user accounts.
Google sign-in information Information made available through Google Sign-In, such as the account identity needed for authentication. To authenticate you when you choose Google Sign-In.

3. Guest Login and Device Identifier

JB STORE offers Guest Login. In the Android app, a device-related identifier is used to recognize the guest account. The current implementation derives an app-scoped SHA-256 value from the Android device identifier and exposes the derived value to the trusted JB STORE web page; the raw Android identifier is not exposed to the website JavaScript.

In a normal web browser, Guest Login uses the authentication service's anonymous sign-in capability.

4. How We Use Information

JB STORE uses the information described above for the following purposes:

  • Creating and authenticating user accounts.
  • Supporting Google Sign-In, email/password login, password reset and Guest Login.
  • Saving and updating your name, mobile number and delivery address.
  • Creating and displaying order records.
  • Communicating order information to JB STORE through the checkout process.
  • Delivering push notifications, including product and offer notifications.
  • Maintaining shopping-cart and favorites functionality.
  • Providing product browsing, search and related website/app functionality.
  • Protecting the service against misuse and maintaining authentication and application security.

5. Order Checkout and WhatsApp

The current checkout flow stores the order in the JB STORE backend and then prepares a WhatsApp message containing order information. When you proceed with this action, the message can include your order ID, name, mobile number, email address, delivery address, product details, quantities, total amount and order date/time.

WhatsApp is a separate third-party service. Information sent through WhatsApp is processed by WhatsApp under its own terms and privacy practices. JB STORE does not control information that WhatsApp retains after you send it.

6. Third-Party Services

The current JB STORE implementation uses or loads the following third-party services/components:

Supabase

Supabase is used for authentication, account management, backend/database functions, order records, product data access, and other backend functionality.

Google Sign-In

If you choose Google Sign-In, Google is used to authenticate your account and provide the identity information necessary for that sign-in.

Firebase Cloud Messaging

JB STORE uses Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications. The Android app obtains an FCM registration token, and the current implementation sends that token to the JB STORE Supabase backend for notification registration.

WhatsApp

WhatsApp may receive information that you choose to send through the JB STORE checkout or contact flow. WhatsApp independently determines its processing and retention practices.

Website content CDNs

The website currently loads some fonts, icons, and JavaScript libraries from third-party content delivery services, including Google Fonts, cdnjs, and jsDelivr. When a browser requests those resources, the relevant provider may receive normal technical request information needed to deliver the resource, such as the request's network address and browser/request details. JB STORE does not use these resources as an advertising or analytics system in the current implementation reviewed.

7. Advertising and Analytics

The current JB STORE implementation does not use advertising SDKs or an analytics service to serve advertisements or track users for advertising purposes.

JB STORE currently offers Cash on Delivery (COD). The current checkout does not collect card, bank-account, UPI, or other online payment credentials through JB STORE.

7. Information Sharing

JB STORE does not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers when required to operate the app and website, including authentication, backend/database services, push notifications and the user-selected WhatsApp checkout process.

We may also disclose information where reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to lawful requests, protect the security of JB STORE, prevent fraud or misuse, or protect users and the service.

8. Permissions and Device Access

The current Android app declares Internet access and Android 13+ notification permission. The reviewed Android manifest does not declare camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, phone-call, or location permissions.

Notifications can be controlled through the Android notification permission and device notification settings. The notification permission is optional and can be denied or disabled through Android settings.

9. Local Storage, Cart and Favorites

The website uses browser storage for functionality. For example, shopping-cart information is stored locally on the device/browser, and favorite product IDs are stored in browser local storage. Selected product information may also be stored temporarily in session storage.

This local storage is used for application functionality and is not itself a payment system or a banking credential store.

10. Security

JB STORE uses HTTPS connections for its hosted web application and backend service endpoints in the current implementation. The Android WebView also restricts certain file and content access capabilities and exposes its native bridges only to trusted JB STORE web pages.

We take reasonable technical measures to protect information, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

11. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as it is needed to operate JB STORE, provide account and order functionality, maintain security, or satisfy applicable legal requirements.

The current account-deletion procedure permanently deletes the authenticated Supabase account and the order records associated with that account. Local cart/favorites/session data handled by the deletion flow is also cleared as part of the account deletion process.

Information already sent to or processed by third-party services, such as Google or WhatsApp, may be retained by those services according to their own policies.

12. Account and Data Deletion

JB STORE provides an account-deletion option inside the account/profile experience. JB STORE also provides an external web page so that a user who no longer has the app installed can request and complete account deletion.

Web account deletion: https://app.jbstore1.workers.dev/delete-account.html

In the current implementation, confirming account deletion calls the JB STORE deletion function, which:

  • Deletes order records associated with the authenticated user.
  • Deletes the user's Supabase authentication account and authentication metadata.
  • Clears the main local cart/favorites/session storage entries used by the JB STORE deletion flow.

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Where a third-party provider separately retains information, that provider's own privacy policy and deletion mechanisms may apply.
