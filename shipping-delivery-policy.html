Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last Updated: September 14, 2026

This Shipping & Delivery Policy explains how JB STORE handles order delivery, delivery areas, Cash on Delivery (COD), delivery attempts, and customer responsibilities for orders placed through the JB STORE website or app.

1. Delivery Area

JB STORE currently provides its own delivery service primarily within Nandyal District and approximately 100–200 km from the store location, subject to delivery feasibility and availability.

Delivery availability may depend on the customer's address, distance, product availability, and other practical delivery conditions.

2. Our Delivery Service

JB STORE currently handles deliveries through its own delivery arrangements. Third-party delivery partners are not currently used for normal JB STORE deliveries.

Delivery timing may vary depending on the customer's location, product availability, order volume, weather, road conditions, and other circumstances that may affect delivery.

3. Order Processing & Delivery Time

Orders are processed after successful order placement and confirmation. JB STORE will make reasonable efforts to deliver orders within the expected time communicated to the customer.

Any delivery estimate is an approximate estimate and is not a guaranteed delivery deadline. Unforeseen circumstances may cause delays.

4. Cash on Delivery — Open & Check Before Payment

Important: For COD orders, the customer can open the delivery box and check the product before making the COD payment.
1. Customer receives the COD parcel.
2. Customer opens the box before payment.
3. Customer checks whether the product is the correct item and appears to be in acceptable condition.
4. If the customer accepts the product, the customer completes the COD payment.

If the customer identifies a wrong, visibly damaged, or apparently defective product during this check, the customer should inform JB STORE immediately and, where possible, before accepting the delivery.

5. Customer Responsibilities

  • Provide a complete and accurate delivery address and reachable contact details.
  • Be available at the agreed or communicated delivery location.
  • Cooperate with the delivery process and product checking procedure.
  • For COD orders, complete payment after accepting the product.
  • Inform JB STORE promptly if there is a delivery or product-related issue.

6. Failed Delivery / Customer Unavailable

If the customer is unavailable at the delivery location, provides an incorrect or incomplete address, cannot be contacted, repeatedly refuses delivery, or cannot complete the applicable COD payment, the delivery may be unsuccessful.

JB STORE may contact the customer to arrange another delivery attempt where reasonably possible. Additional delivery attempts are subject to availability and feasibility.

7. Wrong, Damaged or Defective Product

If a customer notices that the delivered product is wrong, visibly damaged, or apparently defective, the customer should report the issue to JB STORE as soon as possible.

  • For COD orders, customers should preferably report the issue during the open-before-payment check.
  • JB STORE may request order details, photographs, videos, or other information to assess the issue.
  • Any replacement, return, refund, or other resolution will be handled according to the circumstances and applicable law.

8. Delivery Delays

Delivery may occasionally be delayed because of circumstances such as severe weather, road or transport conditions, local restrictions, unexpected events, product availability, or other circumstances beyond reasonable control.

JB STORE will make reasonable efforts to communicate material delivery issues when practical.

9. Order Cancellation

A customer may contact JB STORE to request cancellation before the order is dispatched or the delivery process has started. Cancellation is not guaranteed once the order has entered the delivery process.

For details about returns, refunds, and cancellation, please see our Return & Refund Policy.

10. Delivery Charges

Any applicable delivery charge, if introduced or applicable to a particular order or location, will be communicated to the customer before the order is confirmed.

11. Contact Us

For delivery questions, address corrections, order status, or delivery issues, contact JB STORE using the contact options provided on the website or app.

Email: jbstore01012@gmail.com

WhatsApp and phone support are available through the contact options provided by JB STORE.

12. Related Policies

Please also review our Return & Refund Policy, Terms & Conditions, and Privacy Policy.

COD Delivery Inspection

For eligible Cash on Delivery (COD) orders, the customer may open the package and inspect the product(s) before making payment, subject to the delivery process.

If the customer finds visible damage, a wrong product, a missing item, or another clear order-related issue during inspection, the customer should not complete the payment and should immediately inform the delivery person and contact JB STORE.

Delivery Attempts

JB STORE will make reasonable efforts to deliver orders to the address provided by the customer. Delivery may be delayed or cancelled if the customer is unavailable, the address or contact details are incorrect or incomplete, or delivery cannot reasonably be completed.

The customer is responsible for providing accurate delivery details and being available to receive the order.

Applicable Law

This policy is subject to applicable laws and mandatory consumer rights. Nothing in this policy is intended to limit any right that cannot legally be excluded or restricted.
