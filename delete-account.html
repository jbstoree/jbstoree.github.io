Account & Data Deletion
Permanent action: deleting your account removes your JB STORE authentication account and account-associated order records. This action cannot be undone.
OR

Guest Account Deletion

If you used JB STORE as a Guest and no longer have access to the app, you can request deletion of your Guest Account and associated JB STORE data.

Please include “Guest Account Deletion Request” in your message and, if available, provide the Guest Account/device identifier shown by the app. If you do not know the identifier, simply explain that you used Guest Login and provide the contact details you used with JB STORE.
