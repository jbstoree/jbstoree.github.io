Return & Refund Policy

Last Updated: September 14, 2026

This Return & Refund Policy explains how JB STORE handles product checking, returns, refunds, cancellations, and delivery-related issues for orders placed through the JB STORE website or app.

1. Cash on Delivery — Open & Check Before Payment

JB STORE COD process: The customer can open the delivery box, check the product and verify the item before making the Cash on Delivery (COD) payment.
Step 1: Receive the COD parcel.
Step 2: Open the box before making payment.
Step 3: Check that the product is the correct item and appears to be in acceptable condition.
Step 4: If the customer accepts the product, complete the COD payment.

If the customer identifies a wrong, visibly damaged, or apparently defective product during the pre-payment check, the customer should inform JB STORE immediately and, where possible, before accepting the delivery.

2. Returns After Delivery

Because JB STORE provides an opportunity to check the product before COD payment, normal returns or change-of-mind returns are not accepted after the customer has checked, accepted, and paid for the product.

This does not limit any rights that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable consumer protection or other applicable laws.

3. Wrong, Damaged or Defective Products

If a product is found to be wrong, visibly damaged, or apparently defective, the customer should report the issue to JB STORE as soon as possible.

  • For COD deliveries, customers should preferably report the issue during the open-before-payment check.
  • Customers should provide the order details and clear photos or videos when requested.
  • JB STORE will review the reported issue and determine the appropriate resolution based on the circumstances.
  • Any approved replacement, return, refund, or other resolution will be handled subject to applicable law and JB STORE's assessment of the issue.

4. Change of Mind

Once a customer has checked, accepted, and paid for a COD product, a return or refund will generally not be available simply because the customer changed their mind, no longer wants the product, or prefers a different product.

5. Order Cancellation

Customers may contact JB STORE to request cancellation before the order is dispatched or the delivery process has started. Cancellation requests are not guaranteed once the order has entered the delivery process.

6. Delivery Refusal or Customer Unavailability

If a customer is unavailable, provides an incorrect or incomplete address, repeatedly refuses delivery, or is unable to complete the applicable COD payment, JB STORE may cancel the order or treat the delivery as unsuccessful.

7. Delivery Area

JB STORE currently provides its own delivery service primarily within Nandyal District and approximately 100–200 km from the store location, subject to delivery feasibility and availability.

8. Payment Method

JB STORE currently offers Cash on Delivery (COD) for orders. If online payment options are introduced in the future, this policy may be updated to explain any additional payment, cancellation, return, or refund procedures.

9. Applicable Law

This policy is subject to applicable laws and regulations. Nothing in this policy is intended to remove or restrict any mandatory rights or remedies available to customers under applicable law.
