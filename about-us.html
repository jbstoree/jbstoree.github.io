Our COD Promise

Open → Check → Accept → Pay
For COD orders, customers can open the box and check the product before completing payment. If a wrong, visibly damaged, or apparently defective product is identified during the check, the customer should inform JB STORE immediately and, where possible, before accepting the delivery.
